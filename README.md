## Background
Negotiation is a core business skill and a daily occurrence, especially in today’s global economy where cross-cultural interactions are the norm. As large language models (LLMs) become increasingly prevalent in business applications, the need grows for negotiation systems and agents that recognize, adapt to, and respect diverse cultural values and communication styles.  

Social science research shows that negotiators from different cultures use varied goals and tactics, which can significantly impact negotiation outcomes.  

This project creates a system where both human users and LLM-powered agents can practice, test, and improve negotiation strategies across cultural boundaries. The platform will:  
- Support negotiation communication training.  
- Help employees build cultural competence.  
- Enable companies to develop, benchmark, and ensure their AI negotiation agents are fair, effective, and globally ready.

## Milestones

### Milestone 1: Bot–Bot Interaction Platform (Text vs. Text)
- Build two LLM-powered agents with distinct cultural profiles to negotiate simulated business scenarios (e.g., salary/benefits, school cellphone policy).  
  - **Agent A**: win–win focus, fairness, directness, creative trades, transparent priorities, adaptable.  
  - **Agent B**: avoids losing face, values respect, starts high, gradual concessions, less transparent, adaptive.  
- Build a GUI to visualize negotiation, log outcomes, and display statistics (e.g., # of rounds, deal success, winners, types of offers, deadlocks).  

### Milestone 2: Human–Bot Negotiations (Voice vs. Text)
- Adapt one agent to allow human participation via text input.  
- Enable voice input for human-agent interaction.  
- Collect and compare data with bot–bot negotiations; adapt based on research needs.  

### Milestone 3: Real-Time Multimodal System (Voice vs. Voice)
- Enable voice output for human-agent interaction.  
- Support synchronous, two-way conversation.  
- Provide advanced visualization of negotiation/deliberation flow.  
- Ensure scalability for external studies or classroom deployment.
